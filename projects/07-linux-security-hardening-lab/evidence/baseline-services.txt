Initial Assessment
------------------

No failed services were detected.

AppArmor is active and enabled.

The UFW firewall is currently inactive even though its
systemd service/unit is enabled.

Several services provide additional functionality that may
increase the system attack surface, including GNOME Remote
Desktop, Avahi, CUPS/CUPS-browsed, and ModemManager.

These services were not disabled during the baseline
assessment. Their necessity will be evaluated before any
hardening changes are made.

No service configuration was changed during the baseline
assessment.
