PROJECT 3 — INCIDENT TIMELINE

Initial Alert
An unexpected cron job named security-update was identified in /etc/cron.d.

Initial Finding
The cron job was configured to execute every 5 minutes as root.

Execution Confirmed
Cron logs confirmed that the scheduled command executed successfully.

Artifact Identified
The cron job writes SIMULATED_SECURITY_EVENT to /var/log/security-lab.log.

Evidence Collected
- /etc/cron.d/security-update
- /var/log/security-lab.log
- cron journal entries

Current Assessment
The scheduled task is confirmed to be active and executing with root privileges.
The activity is part of the simulated incident and requires further investigation
before containment or removal.

Status
Investigation in progress.


---------------------------------------------------------------------

Status:

--------------------------------------


Eradication

The suspicious cron configuration was removed after preserving
the original artifact as evidence.

Additional searches of cron configuration confirmed that no
remaining references to security-update or the simulated event
command were present.

Recovery

The cron service remained active and operational after removal
of the simulated persistence mechanism.

Post-containment journal review showed no new
SIMULATED_SECURITY_EVENT executions.

The historical security-lab.log was preserved as evidence.

Recovery Status

VERIFIED — No continued execution of the simulated persistence
mechanism was observed.
